## Escherichia coli

- File: `ecoli-cdc.jpg` (700 x 475).
- Digitally colourised scanning electron micrograph of E. coli O157:H7.
- Credit: CDC / Janice Haney Carr, Public Health Image Library, image #10071.
- Source: https://phil.cdc.gov/Details.aspx?pid=10071
- CDC identifies this image as public domain and requires source and photographer attribution on redistribution. The image is available free of charge from CDC.
- The downloaded image is reproduced without editing or cropping; its original 2 micrometre scale bar is retained.
- The example uses a typical length of about 2 micrometres, not a measurement of every pictured cell. Actual cell lengths vary.
- Primary research reference: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4372735/

## Measurement Note

The two pictures are not shown to the same scale. Physical sizes are approximate. The equalities in the lesson preserve the given numerical values when converting units and notation; they do not imply error-free measurements.
