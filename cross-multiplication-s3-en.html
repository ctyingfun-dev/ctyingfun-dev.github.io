02 · Leading coefficient 1

The product matches. Does the sum?

x2 + bx + c = (x + m)(x + n)
mn = c; m + n = b

Product +6, sum +5: which signs do we need?

The two numbers have the same sign. Their sum is positive, so both are positive.

x2 + 5x + 6 = (x + 2)(x + 3)

2 × 3 = 6; 2 + 3 = 5.

Still product +6, but sum −5: what changes?

The two numbers have the same sign. Their sum is negative, so both are negative.

x2 − 5x + 6 = (x − 2)(x − 3)

(−2) × (−3) = 6; −2 + (−3) = −5.

Product −6: which number gets the minus sign?

The two numbers must have opposite signs. The one with the larger absolute value determines the sign of their sum.

x2 + x − 6
= (x + 3)(x − 2)

x2 − x − 6
= (x − 3)(x + 2)

Incorrect answerx2 − 5x + 6 = (x + 2)(x + 3)

Teacher's noteThe product alone is not enough. These factors give +5x, but the question has −5x. A positive constant does not mean both numbers are positive.

Correct answer(x − 2)(x − 3)

The “sum of two numbers” shortcut applies when the leading coefficient is 1. Otherwise, calculate the full diagonal products.

03 · Other leading coefficients

Does swapping 1 and 3 change the middle term?

For 2x2 + 7x + 3, place 2x and x on the left, and 1 and 3 on the right. But swapping the numbers on the right changes the middle term.

Correct answer2x2 + 7x + 3
= (2x + 1)(x + 3)

For 6, is trying 1 × 6 enough?

6x2 + 11x + 3

You can split 6 as 1 × 6 or 2 × 3. Try each pair before deciding that the expression cannot be factorised.

(2x + 3)(3x + 1)
= 6x2 + 2x + 9x + 3
= 6x2 + 11x + 3

Does 2 + (−1) really check the middle term?

6x2 + x − 2
= (3x + 2)(2x − 1)

The diagonal terms are −3x and 4x, which add to x. Adding 2 and −1 alone does not check the middle term.

04 · Factor out first

What should you take out before drawing the cross?

Every term has a factor of 2. Take it out first?

2x2 + 10x + 12
= 2(x2 + 5x + 6)
= 2(x + 2)(x + 3)

Taking out −1: which signs must change?

−x2 + 5x − 6
= −(x2 − 5x + 6)
= −(x − 2)(x − 3)

When you factor out −1, the sign of every term inside the brackets changes.

Besides 3, is there a shared variable?

3x3 + 15x2 + 18x
= 3x(x2 + 5x + 6)
= 3x(x + 2)(x + 3)

Incorrect answer: the outside factor is missing2x2 + 10x + 12 = (x + 2)(x + 3)

Teacher's noteThe cross factorises only the quadratic inside the brackets. The 2 you took out still belongs in the final answer.

Correct answer2(x + 2)(x + 3)

05 · Zero terms, repeated factors and integer pairs

What if a term is missing, or no pair works?

No middle term: which two terms cancel?

x2 − 9 = (x + 3)(x − 3)

The diagonal terms −3x and 3x cancel. If you recognise a difference of squares, you can use the identity directly.

Identical rows: can we write the answer more briefly?

x2 + 6x + 9 = (x + 3)2

Zero constant: do we need to try pairs at all?

2x2 + 7x = x(2x + 7)

What if every integer pair fails?

x2 + x + 1

The only integer pairs with product 1 are 1 and 1, or −1 and −1. Their sums are 2 and −2, not 1. So this cannot be written as a product of linear factors with integer coefficients.

Factorising is not solving an equation. Without an equals sign, the answer is a product of factors, not a value of x.
