Percentages: what is 100%?

Identify the base, then the multiplier. Equal increases and decreases may not cancel.

Why multiply by 1.15 for a 15% increase?

The original is 100%. Increase: 1 + the rate. Decrease: 1 − the rate.

N = O(1 + r%)
N = O(1 − r%)

O: original; N: new amount. For 15%, r = 15.

Notes: bases and multipliers

A percentage is a fraction out of 100. For example, 35% = 35 ÷ 100 = 0.35, and 0.08 = 8%.

To express one amount as a percentage of another, divide by the reference amount, then multiply by 100%.

18 out of 24 = (18 ÷ 24) × 100% = 75%

Why does a 15% decrease mean × 0.85?

The factor in brackets is the multiplier.

“Increase by 20%” means reach 120% of the original. “Increase to 120%” means the same thing. “Decrease to 20%” means an 80% decrease.

In reverse: how do you recover the original?

After a 15% decrease, an amount is HK$612.00. What was it before?

Given the new amount, divide by the multiplier. Do not add back the same percentage.

O = Nmultiplier

After an increase: 1 + r%. After a decrease: 1 − r%. A 100% decrease gives a zero multiplier, so the original cannot be recovered.

What rise reverses a 20% fall?

A 20% fall leaves 0.8 of the original. The recovery multiplier is 1 ÷ 0.8 = 1.25, so a 25% rise is needed.

How do we repeat the same multiplier?

GrowthV = V0(1 + r%)n

DepreciationV = V0(1 − r%)n

V0 is the initial value, n the number of periods, and r% the fixed rate per period.

15% annual depreciation means × 0.85 each year, not a straight 45% decrease over three years.

Formulas, the three-year example and an unknown rate

For depreciation in value, 0 ≤ r ≤ 100. The same multiplier is applied each period, but the amount lost changes.

A 10% annual depreciation does not mean a 30% loss after 3 years. The remaining fraction is 0.93 = 0.729, so the loss is 27.1%.

What is the laptop worth after three years?

A laptop costs HK$12,000 and depreciates by 15% each year. Find its value after 3 years.

V = 12,000(1 − 0.15)3
= 12,000(0.85)3
= HK$7,369.50

Total depreciation = 12,000 − 7,369.50 = HK$4,630.50.

Find an unknown annual growth rate

A population grows from 20,000 to 24,200 in 2 years at a constant annual rate of r%.

(1 + r%)2 = 24,200 ÷ 20,000 = 1.21
1 + r% = √1.21 = 1.1
r% = 10%
r = 10

The annual growth rate is 10%.
